        Batteries

        From FreeFlight's flight logs. How long a full battery lasts = 100 ÷ the % used per minute in the air. Flights under 5 minutes are too short to measure and are left out of the charts.

        Pack check

        Put a pack in the drone, switch it on and let it link. The battery reports its own health, charge and voltage. The motors never start.

        The pack check reads the battery through Parrot's Olympe SDK, which only runs on x86_64 Linux, so it isn't available on this computer. Your checks from a Linux computer still show below, and the flight-log charts work everywhere.
